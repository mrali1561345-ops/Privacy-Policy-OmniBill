# Privacy Policy for OmniBill

**Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

OmniBill ("we", "our", or "app") is built as a free, lightweight utility tool to help users manage and track personal bills and expenses. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Data Collection & Storage
- OmniBill operates entirely **locally** on your device. 
- We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, financial records, or bill details on external servers or cloud databases. All your entered data stays securely on your device.

### 2. Permissions
- The app does not require sensitive permissions (such as camera, microphone, or precise location) to function. 

### 3. Third-Party Services
- Since our app does not integrate external tracking SDKs or ad networks, your browsing and usage data remain completely private.
